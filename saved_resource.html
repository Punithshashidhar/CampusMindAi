Sustainability Charter

Building a Greener Future at DSU Harohalli

Dayananda Sagar University’s Main Campus in Harohalli blends classical Greco-Roman architectural grandeur with next-generation sustainability standards, fostering an eco-conscious student community.

Solar & Renewable Energy

Over 150 kW rooftop solar photovoltaic arrays installed across School of Engineering and Hostels, supplying up to 35% of daytime electricity consumption.

Zero-Discharge Water Systems

A closed-loop Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) treats 100% of campus greywater, which is recycled for green landscape irrigation and campus horticulture.

Circular Waste Sorting

Strict wet, dry, and e-waste segregation. Organic dining hall scraps are converted into nutrient-rich compost utilized in campus gardens and botanical groves.

AI Proactive Maintenance

CampusMindAi analyzes student reports in real-time, categorizes priority issues, and directly alerts maintenance teams to eliminate resource wastage faster.

How to Reach the Harohalli Campus

Via Namma Metro (Green Line)Alight at Silk Institute Metro Station. University direct shuttles and BMTC/KSRTC feeder buses operate frequently from Silk Institute directly to DSU Harohalli campus entrance.
Via City & KSRTC BusesFrequent buses from Banashankari TTMC and Majestic towards Kanakapura / Harohalli (Routes 211, 213, 214 series). Bus stop is right at the university main gate.
By Private Vehicle / CarTravel south along NH-209 (Kanakapura Main Road). Campus is approximately 35 km from South Bengaluru with multi-level secure parking facilities on campus.
